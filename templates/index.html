Emergency Medical Helplines

Immediate 24/7 crisis and medical response numbers

IF YOU EXPERIENCE ANY OF THE FOLLOWING, SEEK EMERGENCY CARE IMMEDIATELY:
  • Crushing chest pain, chest tightness radiating to jaw or left arm
  • Sudden severe shortness of breath or inability to speak
  • Sudden weakness, facial drooping, or slurred speech (Signs of Stroke)
  • Severe allergic reaction with throat swelling or fainting
  • Uncontrolled bleeding or coughing / vomiting blood
  • Sudden loss of consciousness or seizures

MediGuide Triage Architecture

How our layered safety detection keeps you protected

Level 1 • Emergency

Immediate Care Red Flags

Scans every message for severe respiratory distress, crushing chest pain, stroke symptoms (FAST), uncontrolled bleeding, or toxic ingestion. Immediately stops AI conversation and provides emergency service referral.

Level 2 • Urgent

Prompt Medical Assessment

Identifies symptoms requiring medical review within hours (e.g. high persistent fever with stiff neck, severe abdominal tenderness, pregnancy complications). Advises timely consultation with a doctor or urgent clinic.

Level 3 • Caution

Step-by-Step Symptom Assessment

When common but concerning symptoms appear (headache, fever, cough), the system initiates an interactive multi-step questionnaire to gauge onset, duration, and severity before generating educational guidance.

Level 4 • Routine

General Health & Medicine Information

Answers medication inquiries, cross-checks active database indications, cautions against drug interactions, and delivers structured wellness knowledge powered by Groq LLaMA.

Medical Disclaimer & Privacy

Terms of service and informational scope

1. Educational Purpose Only

MediGuide AI is an artificial intelligence assistant created to provide general medical, pharmaceutical, and health-related educational information. The content generated is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship

Interacting with MediGuide AI does not establish a doctor-patient, pharmacist-client, or any other healthcare provider relationship. Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

3. Emergency Circumstances

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or received from this chatbot. In case of a medical emergency, immediately call 911, 112, or your local emergency response service.

4. Data Privacy & Confidentiality

Consultation sessions are processed securely. We encourage users never to share personal identifiers, Social Security Numbers, or highly sensitive confidential records.
